The rules

Terms & Conditions

1. Product & pricing

Dhirik Chips is sold at ₹15 per pack. The price breakdown is: Material ₹10, Server cost ₹3, Database cost ₹1, Delivery cost ₹1. The delivery cost is already included in the selling price — you will not be charged any additional delivery fee at checkout. For orders of more than 10 packs, the ₹1 delivery component is discounted for every pack in that order; this discount applies only to that order.

2. Delivery

Delivery is available exclusively at Raha College, Raha, Nagaon, Assam — PIN 782103. The delivery address cannot be changed. Orders are hand-delivered by a single delivery agent.

3. Payment — Cash on Delivery (COD)

COD is the only accepted payment method. The payment is due within 7 working days (Monday to Friday, excluding weekends) after the delivery date. If the delivery date has not yet been confirmed, the payment due date will be calculated after delivery confirmation.

4. Late payment interest

If payment is not made by the due date, interest will be charged at 24% per month, prorated daily, calculated as: Outstanding × 0.24 × (elapsed days / 30). Interest is charged only on the outstanding amount and only after the due date, and will be shown separately from the principal.

5. Cancellation

An order can be cancelled only while its status is "Order Placed". Once an order has been delivered or already cancelled, cancellation is not available. Cancelling an order triggers a cancellation notification email; the original invoice data is retained and remains viewable but is stamped as cancelled.

6. Delivery confirmation

Delivery status is recorded only when you explicitly confirm receipt of an order. This confirmation is your own acknowledgement and has not been independently verified by Dhirik Chips. The payment due date is calculated from the date you confirm delivery.

7. Acceptance

By placing an order you confirm that you have read and agree to these Terms & Conditions and the COD payment policy. Your acceptance (with timestamp and terms version) is recorded with each order.

Our story

About Dhirik Chips

Every pack is sold in transparent plastic so you can see exactly what you're getting. We deliver locally at Raha College, and payment is collected in cash after delivery — because trust works both ways.
